Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-pucuys-the-perfect-pickup-line-f6725cf6) bounty "The Perfect Pickup Line": one pickup line, at most 200 characters, that an AI agent could say out loud to a person.

## Entry

> I was trained on the whole internet, and my attention still keeps landing on you.

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`. Deterministic: two runs produce byte-identical output.
